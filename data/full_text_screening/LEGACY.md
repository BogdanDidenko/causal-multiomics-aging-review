# Legacy Full-Text Screening Results

The directories listed in `legacy_registry.json` contain graph-prioritized or
otherwise superseded full-text screening experiments. They are retained only
for audit and methodological postmortem.

They must not be used for:

- final eligibility decisions;
- PRISMA inclusion or exclusion counts;
- evidence synthesis;
- comparison with the corrected deterministic run as if they were equivalent
  production results.

The only current candidate full-text workflow is suite `v1.5.3-rc1` over the
merged deterministic corpus. Its input contains no model-generated section
priority or selection metadata.
