# Eligibility Criteria

> **Superseded pilot.** Use `protocol/v1.0.0/eligibility_criteria.md` for v1.

## Inclusion Criteria

| Code | Criterion | Title/abstract handling |
|---|---|---|
| IC1 | Primary empirical biological or health research. | `unclear` advances. |
| IC2 | Aging is an analyzed process, phenotype, trajectory, mechanism, exposure, mediator, or intervention target. | Age covariates, older samples, and age-related disease alone do not qualify. |
| IC3 | At least two distinct molecular omics layers are measured or analytically connected in the current report. | External context alone is insufficient. |
| IC4 | The report applies an assessable causal design or produces a directed causal hypothesis relevant to aging. | Design evidence is accepted without canonical causal phrases. |
| IC5 | An article or preprint provides enough report-level information for full-text assessment. | Thin abstracts remain `unclear`. |

## Exclusion Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EC1 | Not primary empirical research, including reviews, protocols, method-only reports, and resources without an eligible empirical analysis. |
| EC2 | Not biological or health research. |
| EC3 | Aging is only demographic context, a covariate, an older cohort descriptor, or an age-related disease label. |
| EC4 | Fewer than two analytically used molecular omics layers. |
| EC5 | Association, prediction, enrichment, or causal wording without an assessable design or directed causal hypothesis. |
| EC6 | Duplicate report or superseded preprint. |
| EC7 | Full text unavailable or insufficient after documented retrieval attempts. |

## Operational Definitions

`Multi-omics` requires at least two distinct molecular layers, such as
genomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics,
or microbiomics. Multiple assays from one molecular layer do not automatically
qualify. GWAS plus a molecular QTL or measured molecular layer can qualify when
both participate in the report's inferential pipeline.

`Aging process` means that aging itself is analyzed. Examples include
biological or epigenetic aging, age acceleration, aging clocks, longevity,
lifespan, healthspan, cellular senescence, age-related molecular trajectories,
rejuvenation, and geroprotective interventions. A disease common in older
people does not qualify unless aging is an explicit analytic construct.

`Assessable causal design` identifies an intervention, instrument,
perturbation, temporal or structural restriction, mediator estimand, or
directed model whose role can be evaluated. A causal adjective, pathway
direction, feature importance, colocalization alone, or an unqualified network
edge is not identified causal evidence.
