# Review Question v1.0.0

## Primary question

Which primary empirical studies analyze an aging process or phenotype using at
least two analytically connected molecular omics layers and apply either a
formal causal-effect design or a formal causal-discovery method, and what level
of causal support do they provide?

## Analytic strata

- `causal_hypothesis`: a formal directed or causal-discovery method produces a
  testable causal hypothesis, but identification of an effect is insufficient.
- `causal_evidence`: an intervention, instrument, perturbation, or other
  assessable design supports a causal contrast in a defined biological system.

Causal or mechanistic language without a formal method is not an eligible
causal stratum.

## Population, concept, and context

- Population: humans, animals, cells, tissues, organoids, and other biological
  systems used to investigate aging.
- Concept: a directly analyzed aging construct, at least two distinct molecular
  layers used together, and a formal causal basis.
- Context: primary articles and preprints indexed up to the frozen v1 search
  date, without a lower publication-date restriction.
