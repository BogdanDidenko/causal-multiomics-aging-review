# v0.3.1 fixed-candidate result and role-contract ablation

Date: 2026-09-02

## Decision

Accept the fixed-candidate approach for conditional eligibility routing on the
12-report development sample. Reject `v0.3.1` as a complete causal-evidence
extraction instrument because it failed the prespecified all-field exact
agreement endpoint. Do not run it as production on the remaining 89 reports.

Preserve all prompts, schemas, normalized outputs, and raw Codex CLI logs. The
next ablation will retain fixed candidates and separate eligibility from
descriptive evidence fields.

## Result

- Valid terminal calls: 60/60; no technical retry was required.
- Candidate qualification agreement at three repeats: 45/45 (100%).
- Candidate qualification agreement at five repeats: 45/45 (100%).
- Five-repeat include sensitivity against the analyst draft: 140/140 model
  decisions (100%).
- Five-repeat exclude specificity against the analyst draft: 85/85 model
  decisions (100%).
- All-field agreement at three repeats: 22/45 candidates (48.9%).
- All-field agreement at five repeats: 17/45 candidates (37.8%).
- Exact complete report output at five repeats: 1/12 (8.3%).

The alignment denominators count candidate-repeat decisions. The reference is
an AI-assisted analyst draft pending human verification and is not expert gold.

The fixed scaffold removed candidate-count and anchor-choice variation from the
model response. Remaining failures are concentrated in compound descriptive
judgments, especially what counts as validation of the same causal link and
whether a candidate-level collection of results is positive or mixed.

## Next prospective ablation

Separate the instrument into independently versioned role contracts:

1. eligibility: `qualification` and deterministic first-failed criterion;
2. design identity: `causal_basis`, `design_family`, and `variation_source`;
3. review context: `aging_role` and `multiomics_role`;
4. effect appraisal: `contrast_status`, `assumptions_reviewability`, and
   `result_status`;
5. validation: `validation_strength` under a fixed precedence table.

Each role receives the same complete report and fixed candidate evidence. Each
contract returns only its own closed fields. Python may check cross-role logical
consistency and derive Level, but it may not interpret scientific text. The
ablation must be frozen before model calls and compared with this result.
