# Eligibility criteria contract

Return `yes`, `no`, or `unclear` for each criterion independently. Use `no`
only when the complete report provides affirmative evidence that the criterion
fails. Use `unclear` only when missing or corrupt evidence prevents a decision.

## Criteria

`current_report_attribution`:

- `yes`: the candidate describes work performed and reported in this paper;
- `no`: it is cited prior work, background, a proposal, or future work;
- `unclear`: attribution cannot be established from the source.

`empirical_result`:

- `yes`: this paper reports an empirical result for the candidate, including a
  null result;
- `no`: it reports only a method, plan, hypothesis, or unexecuted analysis;
- `unclear`: execution and result status cannot be established.

`aging_relevance`:

- `yes`: the result concerns aging, lifespan, senescence, age-related disease
  or decline, or directly supports this report's focal aging mechanism;
- `no`: the result has no such relationship;
- `unclear`: the relationship cannot be established.

`named_causal_basis`:

- `yes`: the candidate has a named source of identifying variation or a formal
  directed-hypothesis method under the v0.3 codebook;
- `no`: it consists only of causal wording, association, prediction,
  differential abundance/expression, enrichment, feature importance,
  undirected network analysis, or colocalization alone;
- `unclear`: the method is not described sufficiently to decide.

`multiomics_workflow`:

- `yes`: the candidate belongs to this report's analytically connected
  multi-omics workflow; a one-assay causal test may qualify when it validates a
  candidate generated by that workflow;
- `no`: its omics connection is external context or analytically separate;
- `unclear`: the workflow connection cannot be established.

`distinct_design_unit`:

- `yes`: the candidate is one distinct unit under the v0.3 source/target/system
  boundary;
- `no`: it is only an outcome, molecule, dose, time point, subgroup, validation,
  rescue, or other child contrast of another candidate in the scaffold;
- `unclear`: the unit relationship cannot be established.

Evaluate the six fields separately. Do not return a final qualification or an
exclusion reason; Python applies the frozen criterion order.
